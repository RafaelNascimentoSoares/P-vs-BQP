Implications for Cryptography

This will be a meta section explaining how modern cryptography relies on the assumption that integer factoring is not in P, and how progress in quantum computing threatens cryptographic algorithms such as RSA. I will also mention that this assumption (integer factoring ∉ P) is one reason researchers believe P ≠ BQP, that is, that quantum computers have more power than traditional computers.

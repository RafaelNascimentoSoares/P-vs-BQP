Proposed Outline

1. BQP and P

First, I want to define BQP, and explore the relation between BQP and P. It is trivial that P ⊆ BQP, yet, we don't know whether P ≠ BQP.

2. Shor's Algorithm

I plan to present Shor's algorithm which shows Integer Factorization is in BQP. The fact that we know no algorithm that solves Integer Factorization in P, and that modern cryptography heavily relies on the assumption that factorization is not in P, serves as strong motivation of why most researchers believe P ≠ BQP.

3. Simon's Algorithm

After that, I will describe Simon's algorithm, showing PA ≠ BQPA under some oracle A.

4. Oracle Separation of BQP and PH

Finally, I will briefly comment on the current research frontier by mentioning the work of Ran Raz and Avishay Tal (2018) (BQPA ⊄ PHA under some oracle A) even though I will not be able to dive deeper into that as this is out of scope for this class.

5. BQP and PSPACE

If there is enough time I could also explain BQP ⊆ PSPACE which means P ≠ BQP implies P ≠ PSPACE, which would be a ground-breaking result in TCS.
